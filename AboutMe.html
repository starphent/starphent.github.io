About Me

Hi! I'm Starphent, a game designer, digital artist, streamer, and writer. I have a strong interest in 3D modeling, digital art, sculpting, and painting. I love writing stories and I personally think the strongest and most powerful art one can make is one involving some kind of story. I'm a firm believer that art is you putting some part of your soul and self out there. My favorite kind of stories to read about and write are ones of self discovery, learning about your own identity, and becoming happy with who you are rather than what you will be. My work always aims to relate back to me in some way, if you don't see it immediately that's probably because I haven't gotten to the me portion of the art yet.
